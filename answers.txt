Q3: What is the difference between Merging and Rebasing?

Answer: On the surface level, merging merge the commit history of the two  branches together
without altering their commit history. Meanwhile, a rebase apply the commit history
of the merging branch on top of the current branch so the commit history of the merging branch is altered, 
making the history look more linear from the point of the rebase. The problem with this is that it can lead to confusion 
history if multiple users are working on the same repo when they want to check the commit history.
